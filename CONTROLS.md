# Controls

## Campus

- `WASD`: walk; `R` or Shift: run; `Space`: jump; `M` or left click: mouse look capture; `0`: reset spawn.
- `E`: sit/stand near a chair or enter a game station.
- `F`: fixed SEU facade view matching the supplied exterior photograph; `O`: drone camera around the complete campus; Left/Right arrows orbit, `[`/`]` change height, `+/-` zoom; `V`: top-down layout camera; `L`: room labels; `G`: collider/grid debug; `T`: transformation demo; `Esc`: quit campus.

## Transformation demo

Press `T`, then use arrow keys to translate, `Q/E` to rotate, and `+/-` to scale. Press `T` again to return to the player.

## Games

Game stations open the menu: `1` Tic-Tac-Toe, `2` Rock Paper Scissors, `3` 2048, `4` Rubik’s Cube, `5` Ludo. `Esc` backs out of a game to the menu and from the menu to campus.
