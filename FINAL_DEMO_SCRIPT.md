# Final demo script

1. Build using `BUILD_INSTRUCTIONS.md` and launch `SEU/bin/Debug/SEU-final.exe`.
2. Start at the front driveway, show the garden, IN Gate, OUT Gate, terracotta/concrete/glass SEU facade and signage.
3. Walk through the IN Gate, run with R, jump with Space, and show the collider/grid view with G.
4. Climb and descend Stair 1. Use V to show the top-down layout and L for room labels.
5. Enter the ground floor and point out admission, bank, infirmary, cafeteria, food shops, lifts, stairs, stationery and the nested gaming rooms.
6. Stand by the gaming glass to show the actual driveway/garden exterior through it. Point out the two lights and the rotating gold display.
7. Approach a chair and press E to sit; press E again to stand.
8. Approach a gaming station and press E. Choose each menu item:
   - Tic-Tac-Toe: place alternating X/O moves and show win/draw/reset.
   - Rock Paper Scissors: play several rounds and show score.
   - 2048: make directional moves, show merges and reset.
   - Rubik’s Cube: turn a face, use inverse/reset, and scramble.
   - Ludo: roll, launch a token with a six, move player/CPU turns, and reset.
9. Press Escape to return to campus and confirm WASD movement is restored.
10. Press T for the graphics demonstration: translate with arrows, rotate with Q/E, scale with +/-, then return to the player.
