# PHASE 09 — Tic-Tac-Toe + Rock Paper Scissors

## Objective
Finish two complete mini-games first to validate the game framework.

## Tic-Tac-Toe
Implement:
- 3x3 board,
- mouse or keyboard cell selection,
- legal move validation,
- win/draw detection,
- reset/replay,
- player vs simple AI OR two-player local mode,
- visible turn/status text.

## Rock Paper Scissors
Implement:
- Rock/Paper/Scissors selection,
- CPU random choice,
- correct outcome rules,
- visible round result,
- score tracking,
- replay/reset.

## Integration
- enter from gaming-room stations,
- Esc exits cleanly,
- campus movement disabled during play.

## Acceptance Gate
Both games are fully playable for repeated rounds and return cleanly to the campus.
