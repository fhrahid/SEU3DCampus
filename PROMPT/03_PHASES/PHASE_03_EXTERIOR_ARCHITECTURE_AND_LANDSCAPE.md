# PHASE 03 — SEU Exterior Architecture + Landscape

## Objective
Replace the generic exterior blockout with recognizable SEU-inspired architectural massing while preserving the locked floor plan.

## Implement From References
- gray/off-white concrete masses,
- terracotta/rust-orange vertical element,
- large glazed facade areas,
- right-side vertical core character,
- entrance colonnade/open base character,
- visible Southeast University signage where feasible,
- driveway surfacing,
- garden grass,
- several trees,
- simple landscaping,
- IN/OUT gate structures,
- guard/security exterior character.

## Restrictions
- do not change room coordinates merely to make the facade easier,
- do not invent unsupported wings,
- avoid extremely detailed geometry that harms GLUT performance.

## Acceptance Gate
- floor plan remains unchanged,
- exterior is visually more recognizable as SEU,
- entrance, garden and gate sequence is clear,
- no major z-fighting or transparent-glass rendering corruption.
